How It Works

Six stages, one point of contact.

You see the status of every candidate at every stage. Typical timings depend on the trade and the site location.

Start a Request

Requirement Brief

You send trades, headcount, salary range, start date and site location. We confirm what is realistic.

Day 1

Sourcing & Screening

We search our candidate pool and partner agencies, then verify documents and experience.

Days 2 – 5

Trade Test & Interview

Shortlisted candidates take practical tests. You interview in person or by video and approve.

Days 5 – 10

Visa & Contract
